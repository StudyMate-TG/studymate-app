## Prompt usado

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: transparent PNG mascot variant for the StudyMate streak celebration UI. Image 1 is the edit target, the original StudyMate mascot: an amber yellow notebook with deep navy blue outlines and a graduation cap. Change ONLY the facial expression: replace both filled oval eyes with two short, gently curved closed-eye arcs expressing contented joy; a happy relaxed closed-mouth smile drawn as a simple curved navy line, with no teeth, no open mouth, no tongue. Preserve the exact character identity, original notebook silhouette, binding on the left, cap shape and tassel, proportions, color palette, subtle existing yellow shading, thick rounded navy outlines, and frontal composition. Crisp simple illustration matching the original mark, centered and fully visible, with balanced small transparent margins. Genuine alpha transparency outside the character. Do not add confetti, fireworks, stars, extra props, arms, legs, text, watermark, glow or background; these decorations will be rendered separately in the app. Both eyes visibly closed and smiling, mouth closed with no teeth.
